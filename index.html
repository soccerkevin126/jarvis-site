Jarvis

A personal assistant, built and used by one person.

Jarvis is a private tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps that one person keep on top of their own email, calendar and files: it summarises what has arrived, tidies the inbox, prepares drafts, and answers questions such as "what is on tomorrow?".

Jarvis is not a product or a public service. It has a single user, who is also its developer. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it connects to

With its owner's permission, Jarvis connects to that person's own Google account: Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar. What it does with that access is described in the privacy policy.
