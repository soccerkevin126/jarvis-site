Privacy policy

Jarvis, a personal assistant. Last updated 11 November 2026.

Jarvis is a private tool with one user: the person who built it. This policy describes what Jarvis does with that person's Google data. No one else's data is involved, because no one else can sign in.

What Jarvis can access

  • Gmail: read messages; add labels and archive; create drafts; send a message only after its owner has approved that exact message. Jarvis cannot permanently delete mail.
  • Google Drive: read-only. Find and read files.
  • Google Calendar: read-only. Read calendars and events.

How the data is used

Only to do what the owner asks: summarise mail, point out what looks important, tidy the inbox, prepare drafts, and answer questions about the owner's own files and schedule.

Where the data goes

Jarvis runs on its owner's own computer, and anything it keeps is kept there. To produce summaries and drafts, the text needed for a request is sent to the AI model provider that powers Jarvis (Anthropic) for processing. The data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used for any purpose other than the ones above.

Google's Limited Use requirements

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Jarvis's access at any time in their Google Account under Security, in the list of third-party apps with account access. Deleting the tool's files from the computer removes anything it stored.
